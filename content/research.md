## Microlocal analysis and wave equations

My current research focuses mainly on microlocal methods for non-elliptic partial differential equations. The idea is to analyze them from a phase-space perspective, keeping track of both the position and frequency content of solutions. A typical result gives geometric conditions under which one can solve non-elliptic PDEs in the high-frequency regime and tells us where the singularities of the solution will be located. This is particularly relevant for wave equations, which have oscillatory solutions.

Keywords include: Propagation of Singularities, Radial Set Estimates, Analytic Fredholm Theory, Geometric Optics.
