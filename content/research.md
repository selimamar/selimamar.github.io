## Microlocal analysis and wave equations

My current research concerns analytic methods for non-elliptic partial differential equations and wave-type operators. I use tools from microlocal analysis, functional analysis, and pseudodifferential operator theory.

The notation on these pages supports inline mathematics, such as \(P \in \Psi^m(M)\) and \(\operatorname{WF}(u)\), as well as displayed equations:

\[
(P-\lambda)u=f, \qquad \operatorname{WF}(u) \subset \Sigma,
\qquad \|u\|_{H^s} \leq C\bigl(\|Pu\|_{H^{s-m+1}}+\|u\|_{H^{-N}}\bigr).
\]

## Trapping and spectral theory

My PhD work studies Fredholm estimates for wave-type operators in the presence of normally hyperbolic trapping. The setting connects hyperbolic dynamics with spectral and microlocal questions for non-elliptic operators.

## Geometric PDE

Earlier work considered wave asymptotics on higher-dimensional spacetimes and a peeling theorem for the Weyl tensor. My previous research also includes differential geometry, discrete exterior calculus, and numerical experiments for partial differential equations.
